Ervin Elzie (b. 1994) is a multi-disciplined creative professional based in Auburn, AL.

I’m a results-driven UX/UI and web designer with 5+ years of experience turning ideas into digital experiences that connect with users and elevate brands. My work blends front-end development, user experience research, and visual design, giving me the ability to not only design beautiful interfaces but also build functional, scalable solutions that work in the real world. I’ve delivered 200+ websites, prototypes, and applications for clients across industries, from e-commerce to enterprise portals, using tools like React, TailwindCSS, JavaScript, Figma, and Adobe Creative Suite. I thrive on collaboration, working closely with cross-functional teams to take projects from concept to launch, always with a focus on usability and business impact. Recent projects include: OCV Connect Portal redesign (enterprise UX system), Valyou Furniture Amazon storefront (e-commerce UI), and an Air Force mobile platform. Certified in UX design and front-end development, with a passion for emerging technologies, and digital strategy. Outside of design and code, I’m a photographer, sharpening my eye for visual storytelling.
